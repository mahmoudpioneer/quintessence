## Scope
Swipefile marketing landing page (index.html). Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience: performance marketers at DTC brands. Action: paste an Ad Library link / get the browser extension. Proof: the run-length mechanism shown working on synthetic, labelled ads. No invented customers, metrics, pricing.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The page is a fly-posted street hoarding. Ads that keep getting paid for stay on the wall and weather; one-day tests get pasted over. Refuses the category default: dark dashboard screenshot hero with feature-card rows.
OWN-WORLD: Painted site-green plywood hoarding as ground; ads as two-ink posters (black + fluoro orange, riso blue, or fluoro pink) on cool newsprint, wheatpaste wrinkles, torn edges, stamped posting dates; yellow gaffer tape marks the visitor's pick; condensed wood-type gothic headlines, Franklin-style body, fixed-cell mono day counters.
STORY: Visitor sees a wall of ads with run-length counters, understands "the ones still up are the winners", pastes a link and watches it go up, then sees a pick taped into a brief. Believes: context beats collection. Does: install the extension.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed hoarding. Stencilled SWIPEFILE sign + links top. Right 55%: overlapping wall of 6 synthetic ad posters with DAY counters, one long-runner weathered, one test half pasted over.
