# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: single static HTML/CSS/JS file, no build step. Chosen because this page is a test of the Quintessence skill and must open anywhere and be easy to inspect.

## Users

Performance marketers — media buyers and growth leads at direct-to-consumer brands. They spend part of every week looking for creative ideas that are already working for competitors, then briefing designers and editors on new ad concepts under deadline.

## Product Purpose

Swipefile lets a performance marketer save competitor ads from public ad libraries (Meta, TikTok), tag and organize them, and turn a collection into a brief their creative team can act on. Success: the marketer goes from "what's working in my category?" to a concrete creative brief in one sitting instead of a scattered folder of screenshots.

## Positioning

Assumption (fictional test product): the difference is that the saved ad stays a living record — the save keeps the ad's source, first-seen date and how long it has been running — so a marketer can tell a long-running winner from a one-day test, rather than collecting screenshots with no context.

## Operating Context

Used at a desk alongside Meta Ads Manager, TikTok Creative Center and a team chat / doc tool where briefs get shared. Visitors evaluating the page are skeptical, tool-saturated, and judge quickly.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Assumed for the test: save ads via browser extension, boards and tags, run-length tracking, share a board as a brief. Undecided: pricing, integrations beyond Meta/TikTok, team plans.

## Evidence on Hand

None. Fictional product: there are no real customers, testimonials, logos, metrics, or pricing. The page must not invent them — use honest placeholders or product-mechanism proof (what the tool shows) instead.

## Product Principles

1. Context beats collection: an ad without its run-length and source is a screenshot, not research.
2. Every saved ad should be one step from a brief.
3. Speak the media buyer's language; no generic growth-hype.
