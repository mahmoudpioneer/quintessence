Run-length is the signal

Nobody keeps paying for an ad that doesn't work. Swipefile re-checks every saved ad against the Ad Library each day and keeps the count, so your board sorts itself into four states.

  • Testing

    Day 1–6

    New in the account. Interesting, not proven — save it, don't brief it yet.

  • Scaling

    Day 7–29

    Survived its first week. Watch for new variants of the same hook.

  • Long-runner

    Day 30+

    Someone has paid for this for a month or more. Start your brief here.

  • Pulled

    Stopped

    No longer in the library. Kept on your board with its final count.

From ad library to brief in one sitting

  1. Save

    Browsing the Meta Ad Library or TikTok Creative Center, click the Swipefile button on any ad. It's saved with its source link, first-seen date and the day count so far.

  2. Sort

    Drop ads onto boards by product, competitor or angle. Tag hooks and formats. Boards re-sort by run-length as counts change.

  3. Brief

    Pick the variant that's lasted, add your note, and share the board as a link your designer or editor can open without an account.
